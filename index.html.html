Overview — Key Indicators
STUDY SUMMARY — This dataset includes 60 cases of patients who underwent EEG monitoring (primarily long-term video EEG). The cohort spans ages 7 months to 86 years. The majority of patients presented with suspected or witnessed seizures. A diagnosis change was observed in 38.2% of cases following EEG, highlighting the clinical impact of monitoring. This version merges the two originally-submitted source files: the main 50-variable collection sheet and a standalone "Data.xlsx" extract that carried the only complete values for the Rhythmic/Periodic Patterns (ACNS) field — see the new panel under EEG FINDINGS.
